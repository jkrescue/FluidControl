# P064 candidate → PPO → paired real-CFD chain (preparation only)

This plan authorizes no model load, PPO optimization, CFD execution, or replacement
of the retained successful controller.  Candidate hashes remain unset until both
fixed P064 arms have terminal integrity review and the preregistered A/B comparison
is available.

## Candidate decision and identity

- Preserve the preregistered data-effect comparison exactly: support requires arm B
  to reduce **both pooled development rear-Cl MAE and pooled total-Cd MAE strictly
  versus arm A** on the fixed b01+b03 16-start/80-endpoint H1--H5 panel.  Report K1,
  A and B, every phase and every lead.  B-versus-K1 is relevant adoption evidence,
  but was not the preregistered A/B condition and must not be relabelled as one.
- Before any consumer, require the arm's 256-window/32-update terminal result,
  manifest and seven candidate files, official per-role fresh reload, frozen flow
  and lift-bias checks, source closure and independent terminal review.  Engineering
  fixtures and unfinished candidates are rejected.
- P064 keeps the exact audited `p026_k1` history semantics.  The compatibility delta
  only maps the explicit P064-A/P064-B manifest kinds into that existing runtime;
  P026 K1/K4 behavior and formal dual-control admission remain unchanged.

## One fixed exploratory PPO

Reuse the retained 32768-step configuration byte-for-byte in numerical protocol:
24 deterministic real reset packets, H5, physical 69-D observation, causal 62-force
reward history, seed 20261006, four environments, 128 steps, batch 256, four epochs,
learning rate 3e-4 and final policy only.  Initialize PPO and optimizer fresh; do not
resume or compare intermediate checkpoints.  The only scientific input change is
the reviewed P064 dual manifest.  The FNO remains frozen and must have identical
tensors before/after PPO.

Measured reference runtime is 575.56 s for the prior 32768-step run.  Retain its
12-GiB/no-swap supervision, allocator fraction .06 and Available-memory 50-GiB
startup/22-GiB runtime guards.  A newly trained policy is mandatory because changing
the surrogate changes the PPO environment; the old successful policy is preserved.

## One fixed projected-policy physical test

If separately approved after PPO terminal review, reuse the accepted b00 paired
800-cycle driver and physics.  Apply exactly one wrapper
`0.5 * (pi(o) - pi(R(o)))`, then exactly one existing amplitude/rate filter.  Keep
restart148, dt=.005, control interval .1, paired zero branch, all six windows and the
original primary >=2% drag reduction, rear-Cl RMS ratio <=1.05 and mean-bias ratio
<=.10.  Do not insert a 124-cycle screening run.  Prior paired800 executions took
about 1097--1100 s.  A passing b00 result may motivate one predeclared b01 or b03
replication under a separate approval; each costs about another 18.3 minutes.

The b00 trajectory is training data and b01/b03 are opened development phases, so
none is an untouched test.  The physical result is exploratory evidence for this
specific Re100/L/D5 setting, not broad surrogate acceptance.  A new policy and CFD
result cannot overwrite or relabel the retained three-phase successful controller.

## Formal boundary and elapsed-time budget

Scientific surrogate adoption still requires the unchanged original full formal
suite and all original numerical thresholds.  Historical full-formal runtime was
about 36--37 minutes.  A formal failure does not erase a separately valid real-CFD
observation, but it prevents calling the new surrogate formally accepted.  To keep
the path short, terminal identity plus fixed development comparison may precede one
explicitly exploratory PPO/CFD approval; formal evaluation can run as the adoption
track, never be silently waived.

Expected measured-order budgets are: candidate audit/reload 2--5 min; fixed
development inference under 2 min per candidate (K1 numerical phase was 10.51 s);
PPO about 9.6 min; one paired800 CFD about 18.3 min; formal about 36--37 min.  The
shortest exploratory chain is roughly 30 minutes after candidate review.  Formal
plus one second physical phase brings the evidence chain to roughly 85--90 minutes,
excluding independent review and queueing.  No new architecture, loss, split,
horizon, reward weight, threshold or hyperparameter search is in scope.
