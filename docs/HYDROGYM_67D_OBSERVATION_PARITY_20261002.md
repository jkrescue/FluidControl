# Raw OpenFOAM to HydroGym 67-channel observation parity (2026-10-02)

The tandem HydroGym surrogate policy input is ordered as 32 wake probes × (u,v), rear-cylinder Cd/Cl, and applied rear-cylinder angular speed. This check compares that exact 67-channel layout against original OpenFOAM output, rather than treating Curator HDF5 labels as independent physical measurements.

The read-only validator `scripts/validate_tandem_observation_parity.py` matched validation case `expanded_validation_00` and held-out test case `expanded_test_04` at CFD times 90, 120 and 160. It reads OpenFOAM `wakeProbes`, `forceRear` and the prescribed angular-speed table; independently constructs the same 67-channel layout from Curator HDF5. Across six frames, the largest probe discrepancy was 0.004498 (limit 0.02); rear-force discrepancy was at most 2.22e-7 (limit 1e-4); action discrepancy was zero (limit 1e-5). The probe difference reflects field sampling/interpolation and is not a claim of exact physical equality. Structured reports are `docs/results/tandem_67d_observation_validation_00.json` and `docs/results/tandem_67d_observation_test_04.json`.

The CPU-only, pinned PhysicsNeMo 2.2.2 + HydroGym `4ab9854` container also ran the actual `TandemSurrogateFlow`/official `FlowEnv` reset at validation frames 100 and 400. Direct original OpenFOAM versus actual HydroGym observation differences were, respectively: maximum probe 0.0042062 and 0.0034006; maximum Cd/Cl 4.88e-8 and 9.26e-8; angular speed zero. The existing FNO one-step state/force/reward parity, rate-limit and out-of-support checks still pass. Full runtime reports are `docs/results/tandem_hydrogym_67d_runtime_frame100.json` and `docs/results/tandem_hydrogym_67d_runtime_frame400.json`.

This validates offline observation semantics at sampled times, not a live segmented OpenFOAM control loop, policy performance, grid convergence, or generalization to other Reynolds numbers or geometries. The frozen-policy CFD feedback runner and a matched zero-request-action baseline remain to be built and executed after the 20-epoch surrogate quality review.
