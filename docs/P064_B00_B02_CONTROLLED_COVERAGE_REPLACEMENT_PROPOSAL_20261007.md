# P064 b00/b02 controlled-trajectory coverage replacement — proposal only

Status: preparation only; no training, inference, or CFD is authorized.

## Fixed question

Test one bounded data-composition hypothesis: keep the controlled-data dose at
25% of the same 256-window, 32-update aerodynamic-readout budget, but replace
half of the 64 controlled windows used by arm B with 32 mechanically spaced
b00 windows and 32 mechanically spaced b02 windows. The other 192 windows,
their order contract, fresh K1 initialization, fresh Adam state, learning rate,
eight-window accumulation, loss, clipping, official FNO architecture and
final-only checkpoint rule remain unchanged.

This is **not** a phase-only causal experiment. b00 was generated by the older
K1 projected controller, whereas b02 is generated by the frozen E082 B
symmetry-canonical PPO. Replacing half of the controlled subset jointly changes
flow phase coverage and behavior-policy/state-action coverage. The correct
name is controlled train-trajectory coverage replacement. No extra CFD should
be generated merely to make those behavior policies match.

## Mechanical schedule

- Total: 256 windows and 32 optimizer updates, eight windows per update.
- Original train family: 192 windows, byte-for-byte the arm-B schedule entries.
- b00 controlled: 32 starts selected by the existing inclusive integer rule
  `i*(701-1)//(32-1)` for i=0..31 over legal H100 starts 0..700.
- b02 controlled: 32 starts selected by that identical rule over legal starts
  0..700. The lists are generated before training and stored in metadata; no
  loss, reward, force, or development metric selects a start.
- Controlled total remains 64/256=25%; b00 and b02 each contribute 12.5%.
- b01 and b03 development trajectories never enter training.

The b02 HDF/view must first pass the separate terminal conversion and official
DataPipe checks. Its normalization bytes must equal the parent normalization;
normalization is not refit or blended.

## What this can and cannot change

The producer updates only the aerodynamic readout parameters used by the P064
arm-B experiment. Flow weights and the two frozen bias tensors remain byte
identical to K1. Therefore the candidate may test whether broader controlled
trajectory coverage reduces force-readout error; it cannot credibly claim to
improve the frozen flow field. Field errors must still be reported and are
expected to remain governed by the unchanged flow model.

The fixed 32-update budget is deliberately not extended. Both B and C50 used
all 32 clipped updates, and C50 increased controlled dose while worsening the
fixed development force metrics. That evidence does not establish that 32
updates are insufficient. No post-hoc continuation or best-checkpoint choice
is allowed.

## Evaluation and decision

Use the existing frozen b01+b03 development protocol: the same 16 starts, H1
through H5, precision mode, persistence comparisons and per-phase plus pooled
force/field metrics. b00 and b02 are train-fit descriptions only and cannot be
called held-out tests. Compare the new candidate with arm B under the existing
reported metrics; do not invent a new categorical threshold. Report whether
pooled and each-phase front/rear force errors move consistently, together with
unchanged-flow field metrics and the fixed legacy six-window retention panel.

An improvement would support only the finite claim that replacing part of the
controlled training trajectory coverage helped this readout under the fixed
budget. A null or adverse result would not prove that controlled CFD data are
useless, and no PPO/CFD execution follows automatically.

## Cost and isolation

Reuse the P064 training envelope: official PhysicsNeMo reader/model APIs,
12 GiB MemoryMax, swap 0, allocator fraction 0.06, MemAvailable >=50 GiB at
startup and >=22 GiB during execution, with 20 GiB physical reserve and the
same outer deadline. Expected cost is the same order as the prior 32-update
arms (roughly 19 minutes from the observed C50 run), not a new long-budget
study. A new candidate identity, exclusive output, immutable source closure,
terminal audit and separately approved development inference are required.
