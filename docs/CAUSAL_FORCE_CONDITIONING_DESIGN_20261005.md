Original P013 chunk_force_objective:132-182 packs independent H1/AR time steps into mixed20. That is valid because force output does not feed the next force call. With force conditioning, AR times are dependent. Calling the old chunk function with captured/detached previous predictions would silently replace full recurrence derivatives by stop-gradient training. P020's independent-output cotangent replay is likewise insufficient unchanged.

Preferred scientifically clean protocol: sequential paired H1/AR batch2 at each of100 times, original per-domain/channel/time weighting unchanged (J0 only; do not retain P020 extra statistics). Use exact activation checkpointing of fixed ten-step recurrent blocks, preserving the four-force boundary tensor's autograd connection across every block.
