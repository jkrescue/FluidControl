## Exact offline calculation

Reuse, without modification, `predict`, `reflect_physical69`, and
`projected_request` from
`artifacts/p064_candidate_policy_source_20261006_immutable/scripts/run_p064_candidate_projected_32768_ppo_long_cfd.py`.
For each fixed observation `o` and each final policy compute:

- raw: `r = pi(o)`;
- reflected: `m = pi(R(o))`;
- reflection-odd/deployed request: `a_odd = (r - m) / 2`;

Save all four finite scalars for all 800 rows plus source-row identity. Report

## Source-grounded mismatch

- The training protocol fixes H5 episodes, a 24-reset deterministic panel,
  69 observations, a 62-force reward window and identity VecNormalize in
  `train_p064_candidate_diverse_h5_32768_ppo.py:48-77`; SB3 receives the normal
  environment directly at lines 386-411 and constructs PPO at 445-460.
- Training contains no reflection projection. `Full40CanonicalRewardAudit.step`
  forwards the scalar action to `env.step`, and `TandemFNOStepper.step` applies
  only the amplitude/rate constraints (`tandem_hydrogym.py:754-812`).
- Each reset restores an exact causal 62-force history
  (`full40_canonical_hydrogym.py:63-101`); every H5 surrogate step appends the
  predicted force (`tandem_hydrogym.py:740-748`), so later rewards depend on a
  short policy-conditioned history even though each episode is only five steps.
- Deployment instead calls the deterministic policy on `o` and `R(o)`, takes
  `(pi(o)-pi(R(o)))/2`, then invokes the existing limiter exactly once
  (`run_p064_candidate_projected_32768_ppo_long_cfd.py:92-144,305-325`).

Thus similar training reward histories do not imply similar deployed odd
actions. This is a training/execution interface hypothesis, not evidence that
projection or PPO caused the physical benefit.

## Interpretation and unresolved remedy

This off-policy replay cannot establish physical drag/lift effects, closed-loop
stability, a seed distribution, or causality beyond action-map differences.
The old observations are endogenous to the old policy, and two seeds are not a
robustness study.

Only if the replay shows that seed-to-seed deployment differences are dominated
by large, unstable even components that deployment removes should a later
training remedy even be considered. The remedy is currently **unresolved**.
Naively placing the deterministic `pi(o)-pi(R(o))` projection inside an
environment action wrapper would make the action actually applied to the
environment differ from the action sampled by SB3 and its stored log
probability; that can invalidate the PPO likelihood-ratio update. It is not a
valid single-factor experiment without a separate policy-distribution and
gradient-consistency review.

Any later proposal must first demonstrate an executable, distribution-consistent
symmetry treatment and preserve the fixed B/H5/reset/observation/reward/budget
controls. No custom policy, symmetry augmentation, loss, or architecture is
selected or authorized here. If the matched odd components are already similar,
even that algorithmic review is unsupported; investigate the independently
reviewed physical trajectory instead of retraining.
